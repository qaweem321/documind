# Remote Work Policy

Northwind Labs is a remote-first company. This policy explains where you can work from, the hours we
overlap, and what the company pays for. It applies to all full-time employees. Contractors follow the
terms of their contract instead.

## Where you can work

You can work from anywhere in a country where Northwind Labs has a legal entity: the United States,
Canada, the United Kingdom, Germany and Pakistan. Working from another country for more than 30 days in a
calendar year needs written approval from People Operations at least two weeks in advance, because it can
create tax obligations for you and for the company.

Short trips abroad of up to 30 days a year don't need approval, as long as you stay reachable during your
team's core hours and don't handle customer data on public Wi-Fi without the company VPN.

## Core hours

Every team agrees on four core hours that overlap for all its members, and publishes them in the team
handbook. Outside core hours, you choose when you work. Meetings should fall inside core hours; recurring
meetings outside them need the agreement of everyone invited.

## Home office budget

New employees receive a one-time home office budget of $1,500 (or the local equivalent) for a desk, chair,
monitor and similar equipment. After that, you get $500 every two years to replace worn-out items. Keep the
receipts: purchases are reimbursed through the expense tool like any other expense.

The company also pays up to $60 a month towards your internet connection. A coworking space membership is
covered up to $250 a month if you prefer not to work from home.

## Equipment

Laptops are company property and must be returned within 10 business days after your last day. The company
sends a prepaid shipping box. Equipment bought with the home office budget is yours to keep.
