# Travel and Expense Policy

This policy covers what Northwind Labs reimburses when you spend money for work, and how to claim it.

## Submitting expenses

Submit every expense in the expense tool within 60 days of the purchase, with a photo of the itemized
receipt. Expenses submitted later than 60 days are only reimbursed with your manager's approval.
Reimbursements are paid with the next payroll after approval, usually within two weeks.

Expenses under $75 don't need a receipt if the expense tool shows the card transaction, but you still need
to describe what the money was spent on.

## Approval

Your manager approves expenses up to $2,000. Anything above $2,000, and any single software subscription,
also needs approval from Finance before you buy it.

## Travel

Book flights and hotels through the travel portal, so the company can find you in an emergency. Economy class
is the default. Business class is allowed for flights longer than 8 hours. Hotels should cost no more than
$250 a night in most cities, or $350 a night in London, New York, San Francisco and Zurich.

While travelling you can spend up to $90 a day on meals. Alcohol is not reimbursed, except at team events
approved by a director.

## Team events

Each team has an annual budget of $150 per person for team events such as dinners or offsites. Team leads
can carry over up to half of an unused budget into the next year.
